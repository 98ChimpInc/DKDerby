Frequently asked questions

How does DK Derby work?

DK Derby is a daily confidence calibration game. Each day, you're presented with questions ... but before you see the answer, you commit to a confidence level. The game then shows you how well your confidence matched reality. Play long enough and you'll start to see the gap between what you think you know and what you actually know.

What is confidence calibration?

Calibration is the alignment between how sure you are and how often you're right. A well-calibrated person who says they're 80% confident is correct about 80% of the time. Most people are overconfident ... DK Derby makes that visible.

Why is it called Dunning-Kruger Derby?

The Dunning-Kruger effect is a well-documented cognitive bias where people with limited knowledge in a domain tend to overestimate their competence. DK Derby turns that insight into a daily practice ... not to make you feel bad, but to help you notice the gap and close it over time.

How do I delete my account?

Inside the app, open Settings → Account → Delete Account. This permanently removes your account and all associated data. If you have an active subscription, cancel it via your iPhone Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions before deleting your account.

Prefer email? Write to us at privacy@98chimp.ca and we'll handle the deletion within 7 days.

The app isn't loading or crashes

  • Check your internet connection
  • Make sure you're running the latest version of DK Derby from the App Store
  • Try force-quitting the app and reopening it
  • If the issue persists, email support@98chimp.ca with your iPhone model and iOS version

Is DK Derby suitable for children?

DK Derby is intended for users 13 and older. The questions span general knowledge, science, history, and current events at an adult level. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.
